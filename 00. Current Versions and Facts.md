# Current Versions and Facts

**Verified as at:** 11 July 2026

This note is the single source of truth for volatile facts across the note series: framework versions, requirement numbers, effective dates, and reporting clocks. Topic notes should reference this note rather than pinning their own versions. Always confirm against the named primary source before relying on a figure here, because these values change.

---

## Canonical Facts

### PCI DSS

- v3.2.1 retired 31 March 2024. v4.0 retired 31 December 2024. **v4.0.1 is the only active version.**
- The 51 future-dated v4.0 requirements became mandatory 31 March 2025.

### SOCI Act

- 11 critical infrastructure sectors, 22 asset classes.
- **Three Positive Security Obligations:** asset registration, mandatory cyber incident reporting, and the Critical Infrastructure Risk Management Program (CIRMP).
- The annual board-approved report and attestation is part of the CIRMP obligation, not a fourth obligation.

### CIRMP Rules (LIN 23/006) 2023

- **Four hazard categories:** cyber and information security, personnel, supply chain, and physical security and natural hazards (a single combined category).
- **Five designated cybersecurity frameworks:** Essential Eight (ML1 minimum), NIST CSF, ISO/IEC 27001, AESCSF, and C2M2, plus an equivalent-framework pathway.
- CIS Controls is not a designated framework.

### Financial Accountability Regime (FAR)

- Commenced for ADIs 15 March 2024.
- Commenced for insurers and superannuation trustees 15 March 2025.

### Privacy Act penalties

- Maximum penalty for serious or repeated interferences with privacy: the greater of AUD 50 million, three times the benefit obtained, or 30% of adjusted turnover.
- Introduced by the Privacy Legislation Amendment (Enforcement and Other Measures) Act 2022 (December 2022).
- The Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024 (Royal Assent 10 December 2024) added the statutory tort (10 June 2025), automated decision-making transparency (10 December 2026), the Children's Online Privacy Code (due 10 December 2026), and expanded OAIC enforcement powers.

### Privacy Act small business exemption

- The exemption is not repealed and has no legislated removal date.
- From 1 July 2026, AML/CTF tranche 2 reporting entities (real estate, legal, conveyancing, accounting, trust and company service providers, dealers in precious metals and stones) lose the exemption only for personal information handled for AML/CTF purposes.
- Broader tranche 2 Privacy Act reform is proposed, not law.

### Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) scheme

- Assess a suspected eligible breach **within 30 days**.
- Notify the OAIC and affected individuals **as soon as practicable** once an eligible breach is identified.
- 30 days is the assessment window, not the notification deadline.

### ISM

- Updated quarterly by ASD. Verify the current release at cyber.gov.au.

### Essential Eight

- Four maturity levels (ML0 to ML3). Verify the current maturity model at cyber.gov.au.
- The 2023-2030 Cyber Security Strategy does not mandate Essential Eight maturity levels for all industries. ML2 is the mandated baseline for non-corporate Commonwealth entities.

### PSPF

- Release 2024 abolished the previous 16-policy structure and consolidated the framework into a single annual release with 4-digit numbered requirements across six domains.
- Release 2025 was published 24 July 2025.
- **Release 2026 took effect 1 July 2026 and is the current release.** Verify current requirements against the annual release page.

### Cyber Security Act 2024 ransomware reporting

- Report to ASD via ReportCyber within **72 hours** of payment.
- Applies to entities with annual turnover above AUD 3 million, and to SOCI responsible entities.
- In force 30 May 2025.
- Penalty: 60 penalty units per contravention. A penalty unit is $330 from 7 November 2024, and the body corporate multiplier is 5x, so up to $99,000 for a corporation.

## Reporting-Clock Summary

| Obligation | Trigger | Clock |
| ---------- | ------- | ----- |
| SOCI Act | Cyber incident with a significant impact | 12 hours to ASD |
| SOCI Act | Cyber incident with a relevant impact | 72 hours to ASD |
| APRA CPS 234 | Material information security incident | 72 hours to APRA |
| APRA CPS 234 | Material control weakness | 10 business days to APRA |
| NDB scheme | Suspected eligible data breach | Assess within 30 days |
| NDB scheme | Eligible data breach identified | Notify OAIC and individuals as soon as practicable |
| Cyber Security Act 2024 | Ransomware or extortion payment made | 72 hours to ASD via ReportCyber |
| PSPF | Significant security incident | Report to ASD as soon as practicable |
